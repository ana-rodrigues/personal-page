<h2 className="mdx-h2">Context</h2>

The client, an issuer specialised in thematic and leveraged ETFs, wanted to enter the retail investment app market with a dedicated product. Post-pandemic, the space experienced significant growth, with increasing accessibility to financial markets and a surge in retail investor interest.

They challenged us to create an experience that would attract new investors and help them evolve their investing strategy from buying individual stocks casually to building a long-term, diversified portfolio.

<h2 className="mdx-h2">Role</h2>

This project was developed within a cross-functional team of two designers (including myself), three frontend developers, one backend developer, and one QA engineer. As the lead product designer, I was responsible for:

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">Conducting market research and competitor analysis</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Creating proof-of-concept designs and wireframes</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Collaborating with engineering on technical feasibility</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Researching and collaborating with third-party providers for integrations (e.g., know your customer and identity verification)</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Partnering with the client in the validation of the initial scope and alpha launch plan</li>
</ul>

<h2 className="mdx-h2">Discovery</h2>

<h3 className="mdx-h3">Business opportunity</h3>

Post-pandemic, the retail investment app market experienced significant growth, with much of it coming through mobile apps, driven by improved accessibility, better user experience, zero-commission management offers, ease of payment, and the option to buy fractional shares.

It is also worthwhile to note the growing popularity of thematic funds and ETFs during this bull market period, as—historically—new investing trends are often introduced during periods of high market performance. Funds and ETFs with a tech focus were particularly favoured during this period, as the global rush to digitise services unfolded in the wake of the Covid-19 pandemic.

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">Retail investors accounting for approximately a third of the US stock market trading in 2021. The number of app users increased from 35.6 million in 2017 to over 150 million in 2021.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Interest in thematic ETFs spiked post-pandemic, accounting for 33% of actively managed strategies.</li>
</ul>


<h3 className="mdx-h3">Known challenges</h3>

The opportunity to go to market with a new retail investment app was significant, but so were the challenges. The client was entering a competitive space - especially in the US - with players like Robinhood and Webull leading the way.

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">Highly competitive market with Robinhood and Webull dominating the app space.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">User acquisition was expensive, with a day 1 retention rate of 22% and a 30-day retention rate of 5.8% according to available benchmarks. Achieving a favourable ratio between acquisition costs and lifetime value was key.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Around 60% of users don’t complete app onboarding, due to the length and complexity of the due diligence process, unclear instructions and excessive documentation requirements of financial apps.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Top investing apps in the US had seen significant fluctuations in downloads and revenue, with online trends playing a meaningful role.</li>
</ul>


<h3 className="mdx-h3">Competitive landscape</h3>

We performed market research to identify how the different products addressed different investor profiles - from newcomers to seasoned investors. Our goal was to understand how we could shape an appealing user learning curve.

<h2 className="mdx-h2">Solution</h2>

Based on the research above, we created several design concepts focusing on:

<ol className="mdx-ol">
  <li className="mdx-li">Removing barriers to exploring the app</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Creating engagement opportunities with stock themes</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Simplified investing account application flow</li>
</ol>

<h3 className="mdx-h3">Removing barriers to exploring the app</h3>

We created opportunities for users to explore the app without an account, relying only on the minimum information collected for personalisation purposes. Instead of leading with the account creation journey, we focused on capturing user interests to personalise their discovery experience.

<CustomImage src="/media/unnamed-5.png" alt="User journey when creating an account." />

Pages like the stock detail view are rendered differently based on whether the user is authenticated or not.

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">Without an account, users can only access a limited amount of data.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">With an account, users can access all historical stock performance information.</li>
</ul>

<CustomImage src="/media/unnamed-4.png" alt="The amount of data and the calls to action are shaped differently depending on whether the user has an account or not." />


<h3 className="mdx-h3">Creating engagement opportunities with stock themes</h3>

Newcomers were highly motivated to start investing by soaring interest in specific industries, often discussed in online communities such as Reddit, Twitter, and other forums.

Our hypothesis was that nurturing this motivation would translate into a higher likelihood of completing the full onboarding process and remaining active for longer, so we aimed to increase signup intent by displaying thematic collections of stocks matching the user's interests. 

Themes were made available as ready-made baskets or portfolios, enabling investors to gain diversified exposure to a chosen theme with a single investment.

<CustomImage src="/media/unnamed-2.png" alt="Capturing preferences - users are only asked for highly-valuable information used to personalise their experience before they land on the homepage of the app."/>

<CustomImage src="/media/unnamed-3.png" alt="The user lands on a personalised homepage, suggesting stocks and themes that are the most likely to capture their interest." />


<h3 className="mdx-h3">Simplified verification flow</h3>

New users unaccustomed to the complex requirements of financial apps might be discouraged by a lengthy identity verification flow. We aimed to reduce the number of steps in the Know Your Customer (KYC) process as much as possible.

<CustomImage src="/media/unnamed-6.png" alt="User journey when applying for an investing account." />

<h2 className="mdx-h2">Results</h2>

The collaborative approach between design, engineering, and the client was key to delivering a product that could stand out in such a competitive market.

This project demonstrated the importance of designing for accessibility and engagement in the financial technology space. By prioritising a user-centred approach, simplifying onboarding, and leveraging thematic investment opportunities, we conceived a product designed to lower barriers for new investors and foster long-term engagement through a well-crafted learning curve.

Unfortunately, the product was ultimately scrapped due to the client’s decision to focus on other initiatives. Nevertheless, the insights and strategies developed during this project continue to inform my approach to designing impactful fintech experiences.
