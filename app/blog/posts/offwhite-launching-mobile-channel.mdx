<h2 className="mdx-h2">Context</h2>

By late 2021, Off-White™, under the creative direction of Virgil Abloh, was at the forefront of merging luxury fashion with digital innovation. The brand had already made headlines with immersive digital experiences such as the “Imaginary TV” for its Spring/Summer 2021 collection, which allowed users to explore the collection through a multimedia, channel-flipping interface. 

Building on this momentum and brand ethos of breaking boundaries between streetwear, art, and technology, the brand set out to launch its first dedicated mobile app. The goal was to shape a platform that would not only facilitate commerce, but also extend Off-White’s unique narrative into the mobile space.

<h2 className="mdx-h2">Role</h2>

As part of Gaspard+Bruno's long partnership with Farfetch, we supported them with the experience design and mobile development of the app, through a cross-functional team of designers, developers and QA engineers.

I was responsible for:

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">Conducting market research and competitor analysis.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Creating proof-of-concept designs and wireframes.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Creating the foundational UI kit for the mobile channel.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Assessing technical feasibility with our internal engineering team and Farfetch's platform teams.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Partnering with the Off-White's product and design teams to validate experience design decisions.</li>
</ul>

<h2 className="mdx-h2">Discovery</h2>

<h3 className="mdx-h3">Business opportunity</h3>

The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated the shift to digital, and luxury brands were seeking new ways to connect with audiences beyond traditional retail. Off-White and Farfetch identified multiple opportunities ultimately driving the mobile app launch:

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">A gap for a mobile-first channel for direct-to-consumer sales.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">A growing appetite for exclusive experiences and products, capitalising on the cultural impact of Off-White’s high-profile collaborations and exclusive drops.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">A space for community-building around the brand — mirroring the brand’s multidisciplinary ethos.</li>
</ul>


<h3 className="mdx-h3">Challenges</h3>

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">Off-White’s bold and unconventional visual language and cultural references required a design approach that balanced usability with the brand’s signature edge.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">The app needed to support not just shopping, but also editorial content and unique experiences. It had to provide an adaptable channel for different types of content.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">The app had to integrate with Farfetch’s existing infrastructure.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">The brand had no mobile design guidelines yet, so we were tasked to adapt the recent web redesign and create a UI Kit for the app.</li>
</ul>


<h2 className="mdx-h2">Solution</h2>

Building on the discovery above, we defined a mobile experience that balances brand's unconventional visual language with familiar, frictionless shopping patterns: discovery and editorial content lead the way, while intuitive product, checkout, and login flows support a seamless path from inspiration to purchase.
