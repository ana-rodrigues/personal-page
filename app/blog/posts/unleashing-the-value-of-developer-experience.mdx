<h2 className="mdx-h2">Context</h2>

I joined Swell in early 2023, shortly after the company closed a $20 million Series A funding round. The additional resources were aimed at accelerating adoption and developing an ecosystem of apps, themes, and integrations. 

In the year leading to that round, Swell grew five times in revenue and grew its customer base from 30 to over 1,000 customers. Post the Series A round, the goal was to continue this growth trajectory and strengthen our pipeline towards higher annual recurring revenue (ARR). This meant a stronger focus on attracting customers with complex requirements, with a stronger potential towards higher lifetime value (LTV) for the business.

Our team was tasked with pairing with the Commercial and Customer Success teams to understand how the product experience could support the current growth strategy, especially in terms of acquisition and retention. Together, we identified opportunities to shorten the product onboarding journey and time to value (TTV) for our customers.


<h2 className="mdx-h2">Business impact</h2>

Through this cross-team collaboration, we:

<ul className="mdx-ul">
  <li className="mdx-li">Removed any unnecessary friction and shortened the path to product discovery from 8 to 2 steps, shortening the average time to completion by approximately 50%.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Introduced stronger onboarding progress feedback and a clearer sense of setup completion, aiming for a shorter time to value (TTV).</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Contributed to improved sales efficiency and deal velocity, by nurturing the developer persona, as a key figure of the buying group.</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Contributed to higher developer engagement and advocacy.</li>
</ul>


<h2 className="mdx-h2">Role and team</h2>

The team responsible for this project included one designer, one frontend developer, one QA engineer and me as the Product Manager. We worked closely with Product Leadership, in the figure of the Senior Product Director, and the Commercial team, including our Chief Commercial Officer.

My responsibilities included:

<ul className="mdx-ul">

  <li className="mdx-li">Identifying actionable insights and setting goals</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Driving the initiatives with the cross-functional team</li>

</ul>

<h3 className="mdx-h3">Understanding the buying journey</h3>

At the time, we observed an industry shift from a lead-based sales approach, where contact with key individuals is the primary gateway to qualifying leads, to a much more nuanced account-based approach, where the buying group - not individuals - are the focus of marketing activities.

Our engagement wth customers validated that conversions often involve multiple stakeholders with most of these individuals actively researching and recommending solutions, including technical requirements. 

Playing a role within this buying group are developers, who have become pivotal figures of the B2B technology buying journey, often taking part in the buying group as shadow-buyers - the portion of the buying group that is not directly known to sales. 

<h3 className="mdx-h3">The developer flywheel</h3>

Like other SaaS companies we set ourselves to prioritise the developer experience, recognizing that a streamlined onboarding journey can turn developers into advocates by creating a flywheel effect that accelerates adoption and influences broader buying decisions. Industry leaders like Adyen and Twilio describe this phenomenon as the "developer flywheel", a virtuous cycle where frictionless onboarding empowers developers to become internal advocates, accelerating sales cycles and increasing deal sizes.

<h3 className="mdx-h3">Unleashing the developer's untapped potential</h3>

At Swell, being API-first headless commerce platform, we recognised this trend as a strategic opportunity. While our self-serve storefront builder and hosting solutions attracted a broad user base, it was the customers building custom storefronts - those leveraging Swell’s full API flexibility to create bespoke commerce experiences - that delivered the highest lifetime value (LTV). 

Our Commercial team was eager to tap further into this opportunity, confident that developers played a highly influential role in the buying journey and that our ICP was a strong fit for this opportunity.


<h2 className="mdx-h2">Solution</h2>

To make the most of the developers' untapped potential we:

<ol className="mdx-ol">
  <li className="mdx-li">Optimised the product onboarding journey</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Updated the webapp homepage to guide users better</li>
  <li className="mdx-li">Introduced Sandbox Plus, an extended trial offer for high-value leads</li>
</ol>


<h3 className="mdx-h3">Optimised product onboarding journey</h3>

I paired with our Product Designer to assess the current onboarding journey and identify opportunities to shorten the path to exploring the product. At the time, our onboarding followed the lead of Shopify, using this moment to capture as much information as possible about the user: what type of business it was, what technologies it was using, size of the business, etc. However, this information was not used to personalise the experience to an extent that we could continue to justify it.

We agreed with our Commercial team that we would no longer capture any non-essential information at this point of the journey, offering a shorter path to exploring the product. The effort is shifted from the user to the sales team, who committed to track and monitor new signups using their customer relationship management (CRM) system.

<h3 className="mdx-h3">Updated webapp homepage</h3>

To support a shorter journey from signup to first product value we also revisited the product homepage to guide users through their first steps, providing a stronger sense of progress. Beyond actionable steps and progress cues, we included entry points to the documentation portal and to the developer forum, strengthening the connection between the product and the developer community.

<CustomImage src="/media/case-studies/swell-5.png" alt="Final screens of the dashboard, as developed by our Product Designer." />

<h3 className="mdx-h3">Introducing Sandbox Plus</h3>

Lastly, we used this real-estate to highlight the Sandbox Plus offer, a new extended trial including access to all the pro features. This offer was designed to nurture high-value leads. Prospects could only access this plan through direct contact with a sales representative.

<h2 className="mdx-h2">Results</h2>

This new onboarding experience proposed a faster path to exploring the product, aiming to shorten their time to value and creating more opportunities for developer community engagement. Furthermore, the introduction of the Sandbox Plus offer helped the sales team to identify and nurture high-value leads, matching our ideal customer profile.
